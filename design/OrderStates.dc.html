Order entry, state by state
Every clickable target comes from the engine's own list of legal orders, so an illegal order can never be constructed. Right‑click clears a unit. Esc returns to idle. Tab cycles whatever is still unordered. These six states cover all of movement, retreat and adjustment.
01IDLE
Nothing selected. A fresh movement phase.
Your own units carry a dashed brass ring until they have an order, so what still needs you is visible without reading the list.
02UNIT_SELECTED
You clicked Army Paris. Its legal destinations light up.

MMove
SSupport
CConvoy
Convoy needs an adjacent fleet
Only the four provinces the engine actually permits are clickable. An illegal order cannot be built, because nothing illegal is ever offered.
03SUPPORT_TARGET
Paris is selected, you pressed S, then clicked Army Burgundy.
Burgundy's own legal moves light up, and its province stays clickable for a support‑hold. Hovering Munich previews the hollow‑headed arrow.
04COAST_PICK
You moved Fleet Mid‑Atlantic to Spain, which has two coasts.
north coast
south coast
A fleet must name a coast; the order is F MAO - SPA/SC, never bare Spain. An army moving to Spain never sees this step.
05RETREAT
Burgundy was dislodged by Munich. Only dislodged units are selectable.

Munich is excluded: a unit may not retreat into the province that dislodged it. Occupied and bounced provinces are excluded too. With no option left, the only order is disband.
